# Definition

The organization that everything else belongs to.

An account is the tenancy boundary. Every team, impact, outcome, project, insight, target and measurement belongs to exactly one account, and nothing is ever shared or linked across accounts.

An account is not an organizational concept. Structure inside a company is expressed with nested teams, not with several accounts. One customer, one account.

Because it is a boundary rather than a thing people work on, an account has no state of its own beyond a name and a logo — no progress, no owner, no parent.

An account can have a logo, so the people in it see their own company's brand in the app: beside the product name in the header, in the account menu, and on the invite link a new member opens. Only admins can change it.

# Relationships

Accounts contain one or more teams. A team with no parent team is a top-level team in its account.

Accounts contain every outcome, project, insight, target and measurement reachable through those teams. Each of those belongs to exactly one account.

Accounts contain impacts directly, since an impact has no team to be reached through. Each impact belongs to exactly one account.

Accounts do not nest. There is no parent account.

Accounts are never connected to each other. No entity in one account may reference an entity in another, and that holds for every relationship described in the other specs.
